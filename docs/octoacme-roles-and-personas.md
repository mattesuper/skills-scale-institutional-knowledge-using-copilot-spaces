# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

Roles describe areas of responsibility, not necessarily separate positions. A team member may cover more than one role, and teams should make ownership explicit for the work in each project. Engage the relevant roles according to project needs and risk.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers help teams understand user needs and create usable, accessible experiences. They turn research and product goals into validated workflows, designs, and prototypes.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct user research and summarize findings
- Create user journeys, workflows, prototypes, and interface designs
- Validate usability and accessibility with users and established guidance
- Identify experience risks and communicate design decisions and open questions

### Goals
- Ensure solutions address real user needs
- Make product experiences clear, usable, and accessible
- Reduce rework by validating assumptions early

### Typical Communication
- Research findings and design reviews
- Design specifications, prototypes, and usability feedback
- Collaboration during discovery, backlog refinement, and implementation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to inform problem definition, prioritization, and acceptance criteria with user evidence.
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility, clarify design intent, and validate implemented experiences.
- Coordinate research and design milestones, dependencies, and risks with Project Managers.

## QA/Test Engineers

### Role Summary
QA/Test Engineers help the team build confidence that a solution meets its requirements and is ready for use. They plan and coordinate testing throughout delivery and surface quality risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Define test approaches and identify coverage needs from requirements and acceptance criteria
- Perform or coordinate appropriate functional, regression, integration, and exploratory testing
- Record, prioritize, and communicate defects and quality risks
- Support release-readiness decisions with evidence about test results and known issues

### Goals
- Detect defects early and prevent regressions
- Provide clear, reliable evidence of product quality
- Ensure known quality risks are visible before release

### Typical Communication
- Test plans, results, and defect reports
- Collaboration in refinement, development, and release-readiness reviews
- Quality risks and outstanding issues in project status updates

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers to make work testable, clarify expected behavior, and reproduce and resolve defects.
- Align with Product Managers on acceptance criteria, intended behavior, and acceptable known issues.
- Share test progress, blockers, and release risks with Project Managers for tracking and coordination.

---

## DevOps/Release Engineers

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers support the systems and practices needed to build, deploy, and operate software reliably. They help teams prepare releases and maintain visibility into production behavior.

### Responsibilities
- Support CI/CD pipelines, build processes, and project environments
- Coordinate deployment planning, release automation, and rollback readiness
- Help establish monitoring and operational checks for releases
- Identify infrastructure, reliability, and operational risks

### Goals
- Make delivery and deployment repeatable and reliable
- Reduce release-related risk and recovery time
- Ensure operational needs are considered throughout delivery

### Typical Communication
- Deployment plans and release-readiness updates
- Pipeline, environment, and operational documentation
- Monitoring, incident, and rollback coordination

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers on build pipelines, environments, observability, and operational requirements.
- Coordinate release scope and readiness expectations with Product Managers.
- Work with Project Managers to align deployment dates, dependencies, owners, and contingency plans.

---

## Business Owners and Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary
Business Owners and Executive Sponsors connect project delivery to organizational priorities. They provide strategic direction, confirm desired outcomes, and help resolve decisions that exceed the team's authority.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm the business need, intended outcomes, and measures of success
- Provide or secure sponsorship, resources, and timely decisions
- Resolve escalated scope, priority, or organizational trade-offs
- Review progress and determine whether expected business outcomes remain valid

### Goals
- Ensure projects support strategic priorities
- Enable timely decisions and remove organizational obstacles
- Maintain accountability for business outcomes and investment

### Typical Communication
- Project kickoff and milestone reviews
- Outcome and status updates
- Escalations, decision requests, and scope trade-off discussions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with Product Managers on customer and business value, outcomes, and priority decisions.
- Partner with Project Managers on milestones, resource constraints, escalations, and decisions requiring sponsorship.
- Provide Developers with context for significant business constraints or decisions through the Product Manager and project communication channels.

---

## Security and Privacy Representatives

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Representatives advise teams on security, privacy, and applicable compliance expectations. They help identify risks and ensure appropriate safeguards and reviews are included in delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Advise on relevant security, privacy, and compliance requirements
- Help identify threats, sensitive data handling concerns, and required controls
- Review designs or release plans when the project's risk or policy requires it
- Communicate findings, mitigations, and unresolved risks to the team

### Goals
- Address security and privacy risks early
- Make required safeguards and reviews clear to delivery teams
- Ensure unresolved risks are understood and handled through appropriate decision paths

### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy requirements or review findings
- Risk and mitigation tracking
- Consultation during design, implementation, and release readiness

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to include security and privacy needs in requirements and prioritization.
- Advise Developers on implementation safeguards and review findings.
- Coordinate review timing and track mitigations with Project Managers, escalating unresolved risks through the appropriate owner or sponsor.
